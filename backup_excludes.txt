# --- Firefox/Zen browser profile cache & regenerable data ---
# These are the directories that actually bloat a profile backup; none of
# them are needed to restore your settings, keybindings, cookies, history,
# or bookmarks - they're rebuilt automatically the next time the browser runs.
# Run `python3 main.py backup du zen-profile` on your real profile and check
# the breakdown against this list - profile layouts drift between versions.

**/storage/permanent/chrome
**/gmp-widevinecdm
**/gmp-gmpopenh264
# Deliberately NOT excluding storage/default/moz-extension+++* - that's
# per-extension local storage (browser.storage.local), which is where a
# keyboard-shortcut/config extension could keep settings that don't sync.
# Also NOT excluding sessionstore-backups or zen-sessions-backup - small
# (tens of MB) and it's your actual open-tabs/session history.
